### Temporal, 0 -> 4,603

`lib/Script/builtins/temporal/` is the proposal specification over the ISO
8601 calendar and the UTC and fixed-offset time zones - `Temporal.Now`,
`Instant`, `ZonedDateTime`, `PlainDateTime`, `PlainDate`, `PlainTime`,
`PlainYearMonth`, `PlainMonthDay`, `Duration`, and
`Date.prototype.toTemporalInstant`. Epoch nanoseconds and durations are exact
`__int128` arithmetic, never doubles; strings are the RFC 9557 grammar;
rounding relative to a date is the specification's nudge-window algorithm.
Two places where the draft specification is itself wrong were corrected and
say so in the source: ISODateSurpasses' `months = 0` early return, and
ComputeNudgeWindow's origin shortcut when the starting duration is not zero.
`intl402/Temporal` is not in the runnable set (no Intl calendars or IANA
time zones), and the ShadowRealm/cross-realm files stay skipped.
